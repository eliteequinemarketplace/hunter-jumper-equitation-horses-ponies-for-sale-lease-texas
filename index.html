Pin Oak Charity Horse Show — Houston

The Pin Oak Charity Horse Show, held annually in Houston, is one of the most prestigious hunter jumper shows in the South — a beloved tradition that draws top horses and riders from across Texas and the broader Southern circuit. Pin Oak-experienced horses have proven themselves in one of the region's most competitive and well-attended shows, and horses with Pin Oak competition records are highly regarded in the Texas market.

The Lonestar Circuit

The Lonestar circuit provides Texas-based horses and riders with consistent, high-quality show opportunities throughout the year. Texas's year-round training climate and growing professional equestrian community make it an increasingly important market for quality hunter jumper horse buyers and sellers.
